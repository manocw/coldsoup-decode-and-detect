WHAT THE MODEL IS
  v7 = v6 + the "tempo" feature family.
  - v6 (manocw): 9 behavioural feature families (starter, funding, relays, fields, motifs, hops,
    amounts, history, cycles), two-stage LightGBM, with auxiliary helper targets (--aux).
  - tempo (src/features/tempo.py, written by manocw in the v6 commit but not used in any run until
    now): describes the pace and value pattern of each payment's one-off-pair motif group, linked
    at 10 / 30 / 45 min:
      * group size, minimum / median step gap, gap regularity (CV), span;
      * "tempo" = log(median step / 10 min) and a "slow band" flag (median step 12-45 min,
        no step under 6 min, >= 3 payments);
      * value at each hand-off: share of rising hops, whether every hop loses 8-40% (the
        look-alike pattern), largest / smallest value ratio.
    Why: on train, medium suspicious motifs are SLOWER than their legitimate look-alikes (median
    step 17.8 vs 9.5 min) and look-alikes lose value at every hop, while suspicious motifs do not.
    A model trained mostly on easy (fast) cases learns "fast = suspicious"; tempo states the pace
    explicitly so the model can also learn "slow, regular, value-keeping = suspicious".
  - Nothing else changed: same LightGBM settings, 3 seeds, same stage-2 neighbour pooling.
  - 389 features in total (36 from tempo).

AUXILIARY TARGETS (disclosure, unchanged from v6/v2c)
  Stage 1 also trains two helper models on annotation targets from train/labels.csv:
  "part of a relay cohort" (cohort_id) and "part of a planted non-relay scenario, suspicious or
  look-alike" (scenario_id, family != ambiguous). Targets only, never features; at test time
  nothing from labels.csv is used.
